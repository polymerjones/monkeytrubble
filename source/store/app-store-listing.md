## Description (4000)
Welcome to the troop! Monkey Trubble is a card-matching puzzle about real primates, from capuchins and gibbons to mandrills and golden snub-nosed monkeys.

Flip the cards, find the pairs and clear the pile. But watch the cage: it holds just four cards, and if it fills up, the troop is in trouble.

HOW IT WORKS
• Tap a free card to flip it, then send it to the cage
• Two of the same monkey in the cage clear together
• Cards are locked while another card sits on top of them
• Plan ahead: every deal can be solved, and Retry deals the same level again

TEN WILD CHAPTERS
Travel from The Bridge into the jungle, through The Swamp, The Cloud Forest, The Savannah, The Frozen Mountains, The Lost Temple and more, all the way to the Kingdom of the Canopy. Each chapter has its own painted world, music and new board shapes.

CHAINS AND COMBOS
Clear pairs back to back to build a chain. Long chains earn a combo bonus and change the music.

SPECIAL CARDS
• Joker Caulin: a wildcard that pairs with any card in the cage
• Kevin Kelly, 2019 MVP: his MVP Vision flips every card face-up for a few seconds
• Undo and Shuffle on every level when you need them

CLIMB THE RANKS
Earn XP, keep your daily streak going and rise through the tiers from Bronze to Silverback. Compete on the in-game leaderboard and on Game Center.

MEET THE PRIMATES
Tap a monkey to learn its name, its scientific name and a fun fact about the real species.

SEASONAL SURPRISES
Every October a haunted troop creeps into the jungle with spooky cards, sounds and music.

• Free to play, with no ads
• Progress syncs with iCloud
• Easy mode for a more relaxed game

Monkey Trubble is a single-player card-matching game. No login is needed. Players can pick a leaderboard nickname (offensive names are refused; any player can tap a leaderboard row to Report Name or Hide Player, and reported names are hidden for everyone after three reports).
