# Chapter locations and their primates

Each of the 10 chapters is tied to one real place, so a chapter's card set can share one look and every animal in it really lives there. Use this as the species list when making new card sets. Latin names are given so prompts and card facts can be checked.

The **In the game now** line is what each chapter features today (`CHAPTERS` in `web/index.html`). Where it doesn't match the place, that's noted.

Lemurs (chapter 8) are primates but not monkeys. Great apes live wild only in Africa and on Borneo and Sumatra, so chapters 1, 2, 7 and 8 have no apes.

---

## 1. The Bridge: Central America

**Where:** forest edges and river crossings in Costa Rica and Panama (Corcovado, Manuel Antonio, the Panama Canal forests).
**Environment:** humid lowland forest broken by rivers, clearings and rope bridges; bright and green.

| Monkeys | Latin |
|---|---|
| White-faced capuchin | *Cebus imitator* |
| Geoffroy's spider monkey | *Ateles geoffroyi* |
| Mantled howler | *Alouatta palliata* |
| Central American squirrel monkey | *Saimiri oerstedii* |
| Geoffroy's tamarin | *Saguinus geoffroyi* |
| Panamanian night monkey | *Aotus zonalis* |

**Apes:** none.
**In the game now:** white-faced capuchin, squirrel monkey, spider monkey. Matches.

## 2. Enter the Jungle: the Amazon

**Where:** the Amazon rainforest of Brazil, Peru, Ecuador and Colombia.
**Environment:** dense, tall rainforest with flooded river forest, vines and deep shade.

| Monkeys | Latin |
|---|---|
| Red howler | *Alouatta seniculus* |
| Emperor tamarin | *Saguinus imperator* |
| Pygmy marmoset | *Cebuella pygmaea* |
| Bald uakari | *Cacajao calvus* |
| White-faced saki | *Pithecia pithecia* (northeast Amazon and the Guianas) |
| Common woolly monkey | *Lagothrix lagothricha* |
| Black-capped squirrel monkey | *Saimiri boliviensis* |
| Tufted (brown) capuchin | *Sapajus apella* |

**Apes:** none.
**In the game now:** howler monkey, capuchin, pygmy marmoset. (Until 2026-10-14 it was the black howler and golden lion tamarin, which live south of the Amazon and in Brazil's Atlantic forest.) No emperor tamarin card in the illustrated set yet.

## 3. The Swamp: Borneo

**Where:** peat swamps, mangroves and river forests of Borneo (Kinabatangan River in Sabah, Tanjung Puting in Kalimantan).
**Environment:** brown rivers, mangrove roots, flooded forest, mist over the water.

| Monkeys | Latin |
|---|---|
| Proboscis monkey | *Nasalis larvatus* |
| Long-tailed (crab-eating) macaque | *Macaca fascicularis* |
| Silvered langur | *Trachypithecus cristatus* |
| Southern pig-tailed macaque | *Macaca nemestrina* |
| Maroon langur (red leaf monkey) | *Presbytis rubicunda* |

| Apes | Latin |
|---|---|
| Bornean orangutan | *Pongo pygmaeus* |
| Bornean white-bearded gibbon | *Hylobates albibarbis* |
| Müller's gibbon | *Hylobates muelleri* |

**In the game now:** proboscis monkey, long-tailed macaque, silvered langur, Bornean orangutan. Matches.

## 4. The Cloud Forest: the Virunga volcanoes

**Where:** the Albertine Rift mountains of Rwanda, Uganda and eastern DR Congo (Volcanoes National Park, Bwindi, Nyungwe).
**Environment:** cool, misty mountain forest with bamboo, moss, giant ferns and volcano peaks.

| Monkeys | Latin |
|---|---|
| Golden monkey | *Cercopithecus kandti* |
| L'Hoest's (mountain) monkey | *Allochrocebus lhoesti* |
| Rwenzori black-and-white colobus | *Colobus angolensis ruwenzorii* |
| Blue monkey | *Cercopithecus mitis* |

| Apes | Latin |
|---|---|
| Mountain gorilla | *Gorilla beringei beringei* |
| Eastern chimpanzee | *Pan troglodytes schweinfurthii* |
| Grauer's (eastern lowland) gorilla | *Gorilla beringei graueri* (lower forests of eastern DR Congo) |

**In the game now:** mantled guereza (a black-and-white colobus of the East African highlands), with the mountain guenon and mountain gorilla once their art is in. The snub-nosed monkeys moved to chapter 7 on 2026-10-14. No golden monkey card yet.

## 5. The Savannah: African grasslands

**Where:** the savannas and open woodlands of East Africa (Serengeti, Maasai Mara, Amboseli) and West Africa (Senegal).
**Environment:** golden grass, acacia trees, termite mounds, dust and big skies.

| Monkeys | Latin |
|---|---|
| Olive baboon | *Papio anubis* |
| Yellow baboon | *Papio cynocephalus* |
| Guinea baboon | *Papio papio* (West Africa) |
| Vervet | *Chlorocebus pygerythrus* |
| Patas monkey | *Erythrocebus patas* |
| Gelada | *Theropithecus gelada* (Ethiopian highland grasslands, nearby) |

| Apes | Latin |
|---|---|
| Western chimpanzee | *Pan troglodytes verus* (savanna chimps of Fongoli, Senegal) |

**In the game now:** vervet, patas monkey, olive baboon, chimpanzee. Matches.

## 6. The Deep Jungle: the Congo Basin

**Where:** the rainforests of Gabon, Cameroon, Republic of the Congo and DR Congo.
**Environment:** dark, dense lowland rainforest, swampy clearings (bais), huge buttress roots.

| Monkeys | Latin |
|---|---|

| Drill | *Mandrillus leucophaeus* (Cameroon, Nigeria, Bioko Island) |
| Red colobus | *Piliocolobus* species |
| Grey-cheeked mangabey | *Lophocebus albigena* |
| De Brazza's monkey | *Cercopithecus neglectus* |
| Allen's swamp monkey | *Allenopithecus nigroviridis* |

| Apes | Latin |
|---|---|
| Western lowland gorilla | *Gorilla gorilla gorilla* |
| Central chimpanzee | *Pan troglodytes troglodytes* |
| Bonobo | *Pan paniscus* (DR Congo, south of the Congo River only) |

**In the game now:** mandrill, drill, red colobus, western lowland gorilla, chimpanzee, bonobo. Matches.

## 7. The Frozen Mountains: snowy Asian (and North African) mountains

**Where:** the Japanese Alps (Jigokudani hot springs), the Qinling and Hengduan mountains of China, the eastern Himalaya, and the Atlas Mountains of Morocco.
**Environment:** snow, conifer and bamboo forest, hot springs, rocky slopes.

| Monkeys | Latin |
|---|---|
| Japanese macaque (snow monkey) | *Macaca fuscata* |
| Golden snub-nosed monkey | *Rhinopithecus roxellana* (central China) |
| Yunnan (black-and-white) snub-nosed monkey | *Rhinopithecus bieti* (Yunnan and Tibet, China) |
| Tibetan macaque | *Macaca thibetana* |
| Nepal (Himalayan) gray langur | *Semnopithecus schistaceus* |
| Barbary macaque | *Macaca sylvanus* (Atlas Mountains; the only wild macaque outside Asia) |

**Apes:** none.
**In the game now:** Japanese macaque, golden snub-nosed monkey, Yunnan snub-nosed monkey.

## 8. The Forgotten Island: Madagascar

**Where:** the island of Madagascar: the rainforests of the east (Andasibe, Ranomafana) and the dry spiny forest of the south.
**Environment:** baobabs, spiny forest, red earth, misty eastern rainforest.

| Lemurs | Latin |
|---|---|
| Ring-tailed lemur | *Lemur catta* |
| Verreaux's sifaka | *Propithecus verreauxi* |
| Indri | *Indri indri* |
| Black-and-white ruffed lemur | *Varecia variegata* |
| Red ruffed lemur | *Varecia rubra* |
| Aye-aye | *Daubentonia madagascariensis* |
| Grey mouse lemur | *Microcebus murinus* |

**Monkeys and apes:** none. Madagascar has only lemurs.
**In the game now:** ring-tailed lemur, sifaka, indri. Matches.

## 9. The Lost Temple: South and Southeast Asian temples

**Where:** Angkor in Cambodia, Bali's Uluwatu and Monkey Forest, and the temples of India (Varanasi, Hampi).
**Environment:** stone ruins swallowed by fig roots, carved faces, jungle light.

| Monkeys | Latin |
|---|---|
| Long-tailed macaque | *Macaca fascicularis* (Angkor, Bali) |
| Northern pig-tailed macaque | *Macaca leonina* (Cambodia) |
| Northern plains gray langur | *Semnopithecus entellus* (Indian temples) |
| Rhesus macaque | *Macaca mulatta* (Indian temples) |
| Bonnet macaque | *Macaca radiata* (south Indian temples, Hampi) |
| Dusky leaf monkey | *Trachypithecus obscurus* (Malaysia and Thailand) |

| Apes | Latin |
|---|---|
| Pileated gibbon | *Hylobates pileatus* (reintroduced at Angkor) |

**In the game now:** long-tailed macaque, pig-tailed macaque, dusky leaf monkey. Fits; the pileated gibbon is the obvious ape to add.

## 10. Kingdom of the Canopy: Sumatra

**Where:** the rainforests of northern Sumatra, Indonesia (Gunung Leuser National Park, Bukit Lawang).
**Environment:** the top of a tall rainforest: emergent trees, vines, sunlight above the clouds.

| Monkeys | Latin |
|---|---|
| Thomas's langur | *Presbytis thomasi* |
| Sumatran surili (black-crested) | *Presbytis melalophos* |
| Southern pig-tailed macaque | *Macaca nemestrina* |

| Apes | Latin |
|---|---|
| Sumatran orangutan | *Pongo abelii* |
| Tapanuli orangutan | *Pongo tapanuliensis* (rarest great ape, Batang Toru forest) |
| Siamang | *Symphalangus syndactylus* |
| White-handed (lar) gibbon | *Hylobates lar* |
| Agile gibbon | *Hylobates agilis* |

**In the game now:** white-handed gibbon, long-tailed macaque, with the pig-tailed macaque, Sumatran orangutan and siamang once their art is in. The spider monkey (Americas) and guereza (Africa) were removed on 2026-10-14. No Thomas's langur card yet.
