## 01. The Blood Moon Howler

```text

IMPORTANT CHARACTER DESIGN:
This is NOT a bare skeleton. The character remains a recognizable furry primate whose monstrous cracked ivory skull-like face is organically integrated into its natural fur and species-specific facial anatomy.

Thick charcoal-black and dark crimson fur; recognizable howler anatomy; enormous recessed sockets with fiery amber eyes; massive open jaw and exaggerated canines; dramatic supernatural howling expression.

Thick, individually detailed natural fur surrounds the skull and covers the head, ears as appropriate to the species, neck, shoulders, arms and much of the torso. Approximately 75% of the visible body remains covered in natural fur. The skeletal transformation is concentrated on the face and upper chest. An exposed skeletal rib cage emerges naturally from beneath patches of fur, retaining the species-specific chest features described above. No bloody wounds or realistic gore.

VISUAL STYLE:
Premium cinematic dark-fantasy creature illustration with a highly detailed 3D-rendered appearance. Realistic individual fur strands, richly textured cracked ivory bone, expressive stylized anatomy, sophisticated original creature design, atmospheric depth and dramatic physically believable lighting. Ferocious or eerie yet mischievous and strangely charming, with the particular personality described above. A polished animated fantasy creature, not a flat cartoon or anatomical skeleton.

COLOR SCHEME:
Charcoal black, deep crimson, warm ivory, fiery amber.

COMPOSITION:
Vertical 2:3 portrait aspect ratio. ONE character only. Large expressive head and upper torso dominate approximately 80% of the image. Exposed skeletal chest visible. Keep all defining species features described above clearly visible, including distinctive hair, nose, ears, hands or tail when specified.
```
