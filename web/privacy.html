Monkey Trubble Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Monkey Trubble is a card-matching game by Polymer Jones (Paul Fisher). It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and does not track you. It keeps your progress on your device and in your own iCloud. If you take part in the online leaderboard, it stores a nickname you choose and your scores so other players can see the board.

What stays on your device and in your iCloud

Your game progress is saved on your iPhone and, if iCloud is on, in your iCloud account so it follows you to your other devices. This includes:

  • your level, best times, daily streak, XP and recent level scores;
  • your settings (music, difficulty, volume);
  • a random player ID and a private code the game makes up to recognise your leaderboard entry, and the nickname you chose;
  • players you chose to hide on the leaderboard.

iCloud data is stored by Apple under your Apple ID and handled under Apple's privacy policy. We can't see it.

The game uses your phone's motion sensor only to move the shine on cards and the background as you tilt the phone. That happens on your device; motion data is never stored or sent anywhere.

The online leaderboard

When you enter a nickname, the game posts to our leaderboard after each level you clear. The leaderboard runs on Supabase, which hosts our database. It stores:

  • your random player ID (not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or device);
  • your nickname;
  • your leaderboard score, rating, tier, XP, daily streak and highest level, and when they were last updated;
  • a scrambled (hashed) copy of the private code, so only your game can update your entry;
  • reports you make about another player's nickname, and reports about yours.

Other players can see your nickname, rank, score, tier, streak and troop level on the leaderboard. Please don't use your real full name as your nickname.

Offensive nicknames are refused. Any player can report a nickname; after several reports it's hidden from everyone until that player picks a new one. You can also hide any player on your own phone.

Like most online services, our hosting provider may keep short-term technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the service secure and running. We don't use them to identify or track players.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, the game sends your leaderboard score and highest level to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, and Game Center shows you there under your Game Center nickname. The game also reads that nickname once to suggest it as your in-game name. It's only saved if you keep it. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and you can sign out of it in your iPhone's Settings.

What we don't do

  • No ads, ad networks or ad tracking.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don't collect your email, real name, contacts, photos or location.
  • We don't sell or rent anyone's data, or share it except as described above.

If we ever add ads or anything else that changes this, we'll update this page first, and the App Store listing will say so.

Keeping and deleting your data

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard runs. To have your entry and any reports deleted, email us with your nickname (and roughly where you are on the board) and we'll remove it. Deleting the app removes the progress saved on your phone. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Children

Monkey Trubble isn't designed to collect personal information from anyone, including children. The only thing a player types is a nickname, and we ask that it not be a real name. If you're a parent and want a child's leaderboard entry removed, email us and we'll delete it.
